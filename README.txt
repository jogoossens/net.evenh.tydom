Connect your Delta Dore Tydom to Homey. Works with Tydom 1.0, Tydom 2.0, Tydom Home and Tydom Pro gateways.

Setting it up is easy: go to Devices, tap +, choose Delta Dore Tydom and pick Thermostat or Light. Homey finds your Tydom on your network, and you connect it by briefly pressing the button on your Tydom. You can also sign in with your Tydom mobile app account instead; your password is only used for that and is not stored.

Your Tybox thermostats and TYXIA wireless lights then appear in Homey: current temperature, setpoint, mode, on/off, dim level, battery and fault alarms. New in beta: shutters, door and window sensors, smoke detectors, temperature sensors and pilot-wire heating. These were built and tested against recordings of real Tydom gateways, and feedback from owners is very welcome. Everything runs locally on your home network, fast, private and independent of the cloud.

Then build flows the way only Homey lets you. Trigger a boost when a room can't catch up to its setpoint, end the boost the moment it does. Dim the lights from a presence flow. React to battery and sensor faults. Mode-aware flow cards work the same in heating and cooling season.

Got more than one Tydom, say at home and at a holiday house? Add them all and give each a name; the app settings show the live connection status of every gateway. Your existing Tydom installation keeps running exactly as it did, Homey just becomes the cleverest control surface on top.

Tested on Tydom Home. Tydom 1.0, 2.0 and Pro use the same local connection.
